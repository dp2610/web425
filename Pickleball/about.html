About Pickleball Players

Pickleball Players is a website about learning, playing, and enjoying pickleball with friends. It mixes reviews, images, maps, and personal experiences to show how the sport became a regular part of our group. Lets introduces the people behind the project and explains how our pickleball journey started.

Divy (Founder & Lead Developer)

Divy is the founder and lead developer of Pickleball Players, responsible for building the structure, layout, and overall foundation of the project. He focuses on making the site clear, organized, and easy to use while connecting all the different pages together into one experience. Outside of the website, he also takes on the role of improving everyone’s game by giving tips, strategies, and guidance during matches. He acts like a coach within the group, always helping others understand positioning, serving, and how to play smarter.

Mande (Design & Style Expert)

Mande is the design and style expert of the website, focusing on layout decisions, organization, and how everything visually fits together. He helps plan how pages are structured and makes sure the site looks clean and easy to navigate. In pickleball, he is the most competitive player, constantly joining leagues, organizing games, and pushing the group to play at a higher level. However, playing at such a competitive level sometimes brings frustration, and he can get heated or rages during intense matches.

Chiraayu is the lead photographer for the website, responsible for capturing and editing images that support the visuals across the pages. He makes sure everything has strong visual representation and that the images match the overall concept and feel of the site. In pickleball, he plays as a focused intermediate player who is always trying to improve and perfect his game. Every mistake becomes something he studies, making him a strategic and detail oriented player who is constantly working to get better.

Our Pickleball Timeline

Our pickleball story started as a random idea in a group chat one day and slowly turned into something we kept coming back to. This timeline shows how the game went from a one day activity to tournaments, leagues, and a bigger part of our friend group and something that brought us closer together.

Summer 2023

The First Paddle

It started when one of my friends saw his dad’s pickleball paddle and brought it up in the group chat while we were looking for something new to do. We decided to try it that same day, even though most of us barely knew the rules. The first games were messy, but they were fun enough that we kept wanting to play again.

Late Summer 2023

Playing All Summer

After that first day, pickleball became one of our main summer activities. We played several times a week, learned the basic rules, and slowly figured out serving, rallies, and court positioning. It was still casual, but we started getting competitive with each other seeing each other getting better.

Summer 2024

The Whole Group Joined

By the next summer, almost everyone in the group started coming out to play. It turned into something we would do almost every day, with people rotating in and out of games. This was when pickleball became more than just something random to do, because it became part of our hangout routine.

October 2024

First Intramural League

In October 2024, my roommate and I joined our first intramural pickleball league. This made the game feel more official because we were playing against people outside our friend group. We had to take strategy, communication, consistency and the ability to adapt to new people more seriously.

December 2024

First Tournament Win

By December 2024, we won our first intramural league, which was a big moment for us. It showed that all the casual games and practice actually helped us improve. Winning made pickleball feel more rewarding and pushed us to keep playing competitively. It also made us realize that there was still a lot to learn and that we wanted to keep improving and playing at a higher level.

2025

PKLD and Competitive Play

In 2025, playing slowed down a little, but we still kept pickleball part of our routine. We started joining more competitive leagues at a club called PKLD, which gave us a chance to play stronger opponents. That made us realize there was still a lot to learn. This experince also showed the social importance of pickleball, meeting many new people throughout the tournaments and leagues and a great place to network and connect with other players.

Spring 2026

Second Intramural Win

In Spring 2026, we won our second intramural tournament. This felt like a full circle moment because the game started from one paddle and a random group chat message. Now it has become something we play competitively, socially, and seriously enough to build a whole project around it.
