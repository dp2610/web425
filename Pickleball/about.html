About Pickleball Players

Pickleball Players is a website about learning, playing, and enjoying pickleball with friends. The site mixes reviews, images, maps, and personal experiences to show how the sport became a regular part of our group. This page introduces the people behind the project and explains how our pickleball journey started.

Divy (Website Creator and Strategy Guide)

Divy created the website and helped organize the main idea behind Pickleball Players. His role is to explain the sport in a way that feels clear for beginners, especially through the reviews, map, and guide sections. In the group, he also acts like the coach because he gives tips on serving, positioning, paddle control, and avoiding basic mistakes. His pickleball journey is connected to teaching others while also improving his own game.

Mande (Competitive Player and Content Inspiration)

Mande represents the competitive side of the website because he takes pickleball seriously and pushes everyone to play harder. His life basically revolves around the sport, from practicing shots to playing in tournaments and trying to compete at a higher level. He helps inspire the more advanced parts of the site, especially anything about strategy, movement, and serious gameplay. His journey shows how pickleball can go from a casual activity to something more competitive.

Chirauu (Gear Tester and Improving Player)

Chirauu brings the gear side of the website to life because he has actually seen how equipment can change the way someone plays. He started with a wooden paddle and realized it was making his playstyle worse because it felt heavy and harder to control. After getting a carbon fiber paddle, he started improving and enjoying the game more. His journey fits the review part of the site because it shows how better gear can make someone more involved in pickleball.

Jukeya (Beginner Tester and Entertainment Factor)

Jukeya represents the beginner side of the website because he is still learning the basics of pickleball. He misses a lot of shots, hits the ball into the net, and sometimes sends it way out of bounds, but that also makes him one of the funniest people to play with. He tries nonchalant no-look shots for aura, even though they almost never work. His journey shows that being bad at first is part of the fun and that pickleball is still enjoyable while learning.

Our Pickleball Timeline

Our pickleball story started as a random idea in a group chat and slowly turned into something we kept coming back to. This timeline shows how the game went from a one-day activity to tournaments, leagues, and a bigger part of our friend group.

Summer 2023

The First Paddle

It started when one of my friends saw his dad’s pickleball paddle and brought it up in the group chat while we were looking for something new to do. We decided to try it that same day, even though most of us barely knew the rules. The first games were messy, but they were fun enough that we kept wanting to play again.

Late Summer 2023

Playing All Summer

After that first day, pickleball became one of our main summer activities. We played several times, learned the basic rules, and slowly figured out serving, rallies, and court positioning. It was still casual, but we started getting competitive with each other.

Summer 2024

The Whole Group Joined

By the next summer, almost everyone in the group started coming out to play. It turned into something we would do almost every day, with people rotating in and out of games. This was when pickleball became more than just something random to do, because it became part of how we hung out.

October 2024

First Intramural League

In October 2024, my roommate and I joined our first intramural pickleball league. This made the game feel more official because we were playing against people outside our friend group. We had to take strategy, communication, and consistency more seriously.

December 2024

First Tournament Win

By December 2024, we won our first intramural league, which was a big moment for us. It showed that all the casual games and practice actually helped us improve. Winning made pickleball feel more rewarding and pushed us to keep playing.

2025

PKLD and Competitive Play

In 2025, playing slowed down a little, but we still kept pickleball part of our routine. We started joining more competitive leagues at a club called PKLD, which gave us a chance to play stronger opponents. That made us realize there was still a lot to learn.

Spring 2026

Second Intramural Win

In Spring 2026, we won our second intramural tournament. This felt like a full-circle moment because the game started from one paddle and a random group chat message. Now it has become something we play competitively, socially, and seriously enough to build a whole website around.
