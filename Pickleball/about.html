Divy (Coach)

Divy is the coach of the group and helps teach people how to play pickleball in a simple way. He gives tips on serving, court positioning, paddle control, and how to avoid common beginner mistakes. He tries to make the game less confusing by explaining rules while people are actually playing. His role is important because he helps new players feel more confident instead of overwhelmed.

Mande (Pro)

Mande is the pro player who basically revolves his life around pickleball. He practices often, works on advanced shots, and takes the game seriously enough to compete in tournaments. He is always trying to improve his strategy, movement, and consistency. His profile shows the competitive side of pickleball and how much dedication it takes to play at a higher level.

Chirauu (Intermediate)

Chirauu is an intermediate player who started with a wooden paddle but realized it was holding back his playstyle. After getting a new carbon fiber paddle, he started feeling more control, power, and confidence during games. He is becoming more involved in pickleball because he can actually see himself improving. His profile shows the stage where someone starts taking the sport more seriously but is still learning.

Jukeya (Noob)

Jukeya just started playing pickleball and is still figuring out the basics. He misses a lot of shots, hits the ball into the net, and sometimes sends it way out of bounds. He also tries to do nonchalant no-look shots for aura, but they almost never work. Even though he struggles, he makes the games more fun and shows what the beginner stage really looks like.
