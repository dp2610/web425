Beginner Pickleball Guide

Rules and Scoring

Beginner importance: ★★★★★

Serving starts every point, so it is one of the first rules beginners should learn. The serve should be hit underhand with the paddle moving upward, and the ball must go diagonally into the opposite service box. In doubles, the score uses three numbers: your team score, the other team score, and the server number.

Kitchen and Net Rules

Beginner importance: ★★★★★

The kitchen is the non-volley zone near the net, and it is one of the most important areas to understand. You cannot stand in the kitchen and hit the ball out of the air, but you can enter it after the ball bounces. Near the net, beginners should focus on soft control shots instead of trying to smash everything.

Positioning

Beginner importance: ★★★★☆

Where you stand changes the way you play. The serving line is better for fast reaction shots and returns, the center court area can help with controlled pushes, and the front kitchen line is useful for quick dinks and speed play. A strong beginner habit is moving with purpose instead of getting stuck in the middle.

Paddles

Beginner importance: ★★★★☆

Paddles can change how much power, control, and spin a player gets. Wood paddles are usually cheaper but heavier, while composite and carbon fiber paddles often feel lighter and easier to control. For beginners, a balanced paddle is usually better than one that is only made for power.

Balls

Beginner importance: ★★★☆☆

Pickleball balls may look simple, but indoor and outdoor balls can feel different. Outdoor balls are usually harder and have smaller holes so they can handle wind better, while indoor balls are softer and easier to control. Beginners should pay attention to the ball type because it affects bounce, speed, and how rallies feel.
